Chingu Care AI on Fire TV 1.1.1 brings the TV and the family phone page onto Chingu Care AI's current design, the same as its website and phone apps.

- Every TV screen and the family page use Pretendard type, the ripple's thin arcs, round family portraits and photographs of the parent's home.
- At the two larger text sizes the home screen, the check-in's answers and long postcards scroll with the remote and say when there is more below.
- The family screen counts new postcards the way the home screen does, and opening a postcard updates its New badge and both counts at once.
- The app carries OkHttp 4.12.1 or later, and every build is checked for it.

The APK holds all four Android chip types and looks for the care hub at localhost:8797. Start the hub on your computer (see the README), then `adb install chingu-care-ai-tv-1.1.1.apk` and `adb reverse tcp:8797 tcp:8797` on a Fire TV or Android TV device with ADB debugging on. To build against a hub elsewhere on your network, use `HUB_URL` with `./run-demo.sh`.
